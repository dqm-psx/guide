    02Find parents

    Find base pairings and conditional recipes for your chosen offspring.

    Base table pairs, confirmed + value recipes, and title-screen Breeding room recipes are listed in labeled groups. Use “Try pair” to inspect both directions in Find a pairing.

    Pedigree + Mate → offspring

    Ordered parent combinations for the desired offspring
    PedigreeMateInspect pairing

    A superscript + beside a parent points to the condition in that row. Either parent meeting the listed + value is sufficient; their values are not added together. Breeding room recipes apply only to two saves through Breeding on the title screen, where each player’s monster is their own pedigree.

    Pinned targets and their breeding plans live in My game, in the Targets & plans tab. Choose an offspring above, pin it, then open its plan there.

    03Breeding table

    Browse all species combinations. Filter either axis, set the parents’ + values, and choose a breeding context.

    Pedigrees · rows
    Mates · columns

    Rows are pedigrees; columns are mates. Reversing the parents can change the result. + values apply to every parent on their axis; they are checked separately, never added together. Choose a result to inspect both directions. Breeding eligibility is not checked.

    The two-save Breeding room is accessed from the title screen. A matching room rule takes precedence over + rules; each player uses their own monster as pedigree.

    Full English names identify the monster; abbreviated in-game names help you find it in menus. Numbered forms distinguish entries with the same native name. Names and recipes may differ in other localizations or games. Extra and internal slots are hidden by default; appearing in the data does not establish that they are obtainable.
